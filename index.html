Branda Invoice Collector

Branda Invoice Collector is an internal tool used by Branda to collect invoices that arrive in the company's own Gmail mailboxes. Once a month it finds the invoices received in the previous month, labels them in Gmail, and saves copies to the company's Google Drive.

The tool is used only by Branda for its own mailboxes. It is not offered to the public.
