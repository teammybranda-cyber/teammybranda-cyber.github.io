Privacy Policy

Branda Invoice Collector. Last updated: October 13, 2026.

What the app accesses

  • Gmail: reads emails in mailboxes that Branda has authorized, to find invoices and their attachments, and adds a label to emails identified as invoices.
  • Google Drive: creates folders and saves invoice files and a monthly report in Branda's Drive.

How the data is used

Data is used only to collect and organize Branda's own invoices. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Email content may be processed by an AI assistant used by Branda solely to decide which emails are invoices.

Storage

Authorization tokens are stored privately by Branda and are used only to run the monthly invoice collection. Invoice files are stored in Branda's Google Drive.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Branda Invoice Collector's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
